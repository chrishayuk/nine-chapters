What it found

The answers are written over the board's divisor (法) after the divisor and every dividend (實) are reduced together (約): that reading writes all 11 fractional answers as the text does; the 法 as the board leaves it writes 7, and lowest terms 9. The text writes 1 18/52, not 1 9/26, and 33/111, not 11/37.

In this edition, Problem 6 sets its 實 as 正 where the answer needs 負; run as printed, the board gives −8 and −3.

Dai Zhen's 145 算 for the plain method is reproduced exactly by one rule of counting (every multiplication, taking and division of one figure, the cancelling heads left out); his 99 only by a rule proposed after both were known. Liu Hui's working "made short", which he says takes 77 算, was run as printed in 100,000,000 readings: none reaches the price of 黍.

The two published reconstructions of it both run. Guo Shuchun's emended text (算 as operations) reaches the prices, and his rule counts 67 of the 77, leaving 10 for placing rods (guo-18-old). All fifteen of Hart's rod counts (算 as rods) agree with the board, and his 77 holds only if each step is worked a position at a time from the bottom; from the top it needs 78 (hart-18-old). Each gives 77 only on its own path.
