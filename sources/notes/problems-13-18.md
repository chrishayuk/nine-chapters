# Problems 8.13-8.18: transcription notes

Every figure was read from the scans (`sources/sibu-congkan/pages/p22.jpg`-`p31.jpg`, and `p43`-`p45`
for Dai Zhen). The paged Wikisource text (`卷第八.paged.txt`) was used to find my place. The punctuated
Wikisource text (`sources/wikisource/jiuzhang.wiki`) was read as a third witness.

Files: `corpus/no-13.jz` … `corpus/no-18.jz`, `corpus/dai-zhen-13.jz`. Liu Hui's figure for 13 is on
`no-13.jz` itself (TRANSCRIBING.md item 8), so there is no separate `liu-hui-13.jz`.

## 8.13 五家共井 (pp. 22-23; Dai Zhen pp. 43-45)

- The image and the paged text agree on the statement, the answer (721, 265, 191, 148, 129, 76 寸), the 術
  and Liu Hui's note. I found no unclear characters.
- jiuzhang.wiki reads 「如各得所不足一綆」. The scan has no 如: 「各得所不足一綆皆逮」.
- The board: the text does not say how to set it. I made the depth the 實 of every column (Liu Hui:
  「名各一逮井」). Each condition 「X n 綆不足如 Y 一綆」 is set as n·X + Y = 1 depth. The columns follow
  the text's order, 甲乙 on the right. The unit is one well-depth. The answer is a `Rates` line: the
  法 721 is the depth, and the 實s are the ropes. ✓ as the board leaves them; no reducing was needed.
- Liu Hui: 「其後法得七百二十一實七十六」. After the 甲 to 丁 positions are cleared, the left-hand column
  is the one left holding only 戊 and the 實. I wrote it as `Gives left 0 0 0 0 721 76`. ✓
- Dai Zhen (`dai-zhen-13.jz`):
  - p. 44 gives the half-depth and double-depth readings. Half: 三丈六尺有半寸, with ropes 一丈三尺二寸半,
    九尺五寸半, 七尺四寸, 六尺四寸半, 三尺八寸. Double: 十四丈四尺二寸, with ropes 五丈三尺, 三丈八尺二寸,
    二丈九尺六寸, 二丈五尺八寸, 一丈五尺二寸. These are not board figures, so I kept them as words; by
    hand they are exactly half and double the text's figures.
  - He gives his answer as rates: 721, 265, 191, 148, 129, 76. I wrote this as `Rates`. ✓
  - p. 45 says 「此上訛舛不可通」 of Liu Hui's sentence. He sets 721 as each column's 下實 and works the
    board again (重求之). He says this gives 法 721 and 實 五萬四千七百九十六, and 54796 ÷ 721 = 76. I
    wrote it as a second board, `Gives left 0 0 0 0 721 54796`. ✓
  - The paged text agrees with the image on pp. 43-45 for these figures.

## 8.14 白靑黃黑禾 (pp. 23-25)

- The image and the paged text agree. jiuzhang.wiki writes 黑禾 …之一十 and 黃 …之一十七. The scan has
  之十 and 之十七; this is a different edition.
- 黑禾's answer is the last column on p. 24 and ends 「分斗之十」. p. 25 begins with the 術.
- The board: 「各置所取」. Each grain's own bu, plus one bu of each of the two grains it takes, equals 1 斗.
  The columns run 白 (right), 靑, 黃, 黑 (left). The unit is the 斗.
- jiuzhang: ✓. Written over 111: the 法 and 實s divided by what they share. The board leaves 198/666; in
  lowest terms it would be 11/37.

## 8.15 甲乙丙禾 重過於石 (pp. 25-26)

- The image and the paged text agree, including Liu Hui's two notes. Neither note states figures.
- The board: 「置重過於石之物爲負」. 「甲二重如乙一」 reads 2甲 − 1乙 = 1 石, so the other grain is set
  negative. The columns are right 2 −1 0 1, middle 0 3 −1 1, left −1 0 4 1. The unit is the 石.
- jiuzhang: ✓. Written over 23, the 法 as the board leaves it.

## 8.17 羊犬雞兔 (pp. 27-28)

- **Disagreement with the paged text.** The scan has 兔 (hare) throughout. Its statement reads
  「五羊四犬三雞二兔直錢一千四百九十六，四羊二犬六雞三兔直錢一千一百七十五，三羊一犬七雞五兔直錢
  九百五十八，二羊三犬五雞一兔直錢八百六十一，問羊犬雞兔價各幾何」, and its answer ends 「兔價二十九」.
  The paged text drops 兔 and 直 throughout (「三雞二錢一千四百九十六」, 「問羊犬雞價」, 「價二十九」).
  jiuzhang.wiki agrees with the scan.
- The board: one column per purchase, in the text's order, all positive. The unit is the 錢.
- jiuzhang: ✓ (177, 121, 23, 29).

## 8.18 麻麥菽荅黍 (pp. 28-30)

- **Disagreement with the paged text.** The scan has 直錢 in each of the five purchases and in
  「問一斗直幾何」. The paged text has only 錢, and 「問一斗幾何」.
- The fourth purchase writes 答九斗 (with the bamboo radical) in the scan; the others have 荅. I kept it as
  the scan has it, and the paged text agrees.
- The scan's 黍 is given by the paged text as 𮮐. I transcribed it as 黍, the character jiuzhang.wiki
  also uses.
- The board: one column per purchase, in the text's order. The unit is the 錢.
- jiuzhang: ✓ (7, 4, 3, 5, 6).
- I did not transcribe Liu Hui's commentary (pp. 30-35) or Dai Zhen's notes on it, as instructed. The 術
  is 「術曰如方程以正負術入之」 (p. 30), and the commentary begins 「此麻麥與均輸…」 straight after it.

## For the reader

- Only 8.15 has a negative setting in its 術. The 術s of 8.16 and 8.17 say only 以正負術入之, and every
  figure in their statements is positive.
- The notation expressed everything except Dai Zhen's half and double depths, which are alternative
  answers in 丈尺, not board figures. I kept them as quoted words.
