## The 直 of 直除 (Problem 1, and throughout)

The image reads **而以直除** (p. 3, last column of main text), **又乘其次亦以直除**
(p. 4), **而以直除** (p. 4, after 徧乘左行), and Liu Hui's 列此下禾之秉實乘兩行以
**直**除 (p. 4). Wikisource drops 直 every time (而以除, 亦以除),
presumably because the character was not recognised. Dai Zhen's 訂訛 (p. 36)
also reads 而以直除 / 亦以直除, and the third witness has 直 too.

Note for the project: `jiuzhang decisions` item 3 says "The Sibu congkan text
(from the Weiboxie edition) reads 而以除, without 直". The scan does not: it
reads 直. That statement rests on the Wikisource transcription. (Not edited:
outside my files.)

## No. 8.1 (pp. 2-6)

- Statement p. 2, answer and procedure pp. 3-6. Figures 3 2 1 39 / 2 3 1 34 /
  1 2 3 26, answers 9 1/4, 4 1/4, 2 3/4: image and Wikisource agree.
- Board: set as the procedure says. The first steps are written out:
  徧乘 middle by the head of right, 直除; 又乘其次 (left by the head of right),
  亦以直除; 中行中禾 徧乘左行, 而以直除. The board then holds left 0 0 36 99,
  and `Solve` finishes (法 36).
- 其次 is read as the next column, the left; Liu Hui's 復去左行首 supports it.
- Gives lines: none. Neither the text, Liu Hui, nor Li Chunfeng states a figure
  of any column. Liu Hui's long commentary (pp. 3-6) explains 齊同, why
  taking rows from rows keeps the 率, and the back-substitution (以法乘中行下實
  ...), all without figures.
- Dai Zhen (p. 36): 直 and 值 are used interchangeably; 直除 means 對減. He reads
  以右行上禾徧乘中行 as also 復以中行上禾徧乘右行, then subtract: i.e. mutual
  multiplication (`crosswise`), the text naming one multiplication 古人文省;
  likewise 又乘其次. He states no figures, so there is no `dai-zhen-NN.jz`
  page for this range. Recorded on the page as a `%` note, not as a reading:
  his reading would replace both the Multiply line and the Take line with one
  `Take ... crosswise`, which the `|` notation (one alternative per line)
  cannot express across two lines.
- jiuzhang: Answer ✓, "written over 4: the 法 and 實s divided by what they
  share (約), 4; or each in its lowest terms (not as the board leaves it,
  9 9/36)".
- Other differences, image vs Wikisource: none in figures. (Dai Zhen p. 37:
  the image has 同名伯除也, as Wikisource does; 伯 for 相, an error of the
  print, outside my problems.)

## No. 8.6 (pp. 13-14)

- Board as the procedure sets it: 置上禾三秉正，下禾十秉負，益實六斗正，次置上禾
  二秉負，下禾五秉正，益實一斗正: right 3 -10 6, left -2 5 1. Unit 斗.
- **jiuzhang: Answer 8, 3 ✗ "the board gives -8 for 8, -3 for 3"** (法 -5,
  實 40 15). Re-read the image (p. 14, cropped and enlarged): both 益實 are
  followed by 正. Wikisource agrees (正, 正). Left as the scan has it.
- The third witness reads 益實六斗**負** ... 益實一斗**負**; with those signs
  (`jiuzhang solve 3 -10 -6 / -2 5 -1`) the board gives 法 -5, 實 -40 -15,
  i.e. 8, 3. The problem's sense agrees: 3 上 + 6 = 10 下, so with 上 正 and 下
  負 the 實 is -6. The scan's 正 is the sign that disagrees.
- Liu Hui: 言上禾三秉之實少，益其六斗，然後於下禾十秉相當也，故亦互其算，而以
  六斗爲差實，差實者下禾之餘實 (p. 14). No figures beyond the 6 斗.
